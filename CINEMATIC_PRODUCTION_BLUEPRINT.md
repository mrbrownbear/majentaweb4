# Majenta — Information made tangible

## Intent
A single persistent architectural world reveals the relationship between disciplined engineering information and coordinated physical space. Preserve the exact user SVG. The source building was generated by Hyper3D and downloaded successfully. Its single indexed mesh must be partitioned by original surface location for reversible assembly and typography animation; no generic building substitute is permitted.

## Runtime
Three.js WebGL with PBR material textures, ACES, GSAP ScrollTrigger and Lenis. 1100vh scroll. Each frame evaluates deterministic world state from absolute normalized scroll, so reverse scrolling, anchor jumps, resizing and reload at depth produce the same result. Original model bounds normalize to a 12-unit high pavilion; retain aspect ratio. Partition indexed source triangles into stable spatial component groups preserving positions, normals and UVs. Store source transforms, construction offsets, explosion offsets and final glyph targets. For finale, original surface groups translate/rotate/scale into a seven-letter MAJENTA bitmap stroke map, with controlled material tint. No HTML replacement for the final sculpture.

## Sequence
1. The drawing: Top-down navy floorplans plot on white with structural grids, dimensions and section marks
2. The plan becomes space: Walls extrude, columns and slabs emerge; camera tilts to perspective
3. Structural assembly: Steel, cores, stairs and roof assemble with slow orbit
4. The facade: Glazing and fins install sequentially; landscape emerges in warm three-quarter hero
5. Approach: Dolly past planting and facade foreground
6. The entrance: Doors retract and camera crosses threshold into warm stone lobby
7. The atrium: Move through double-height atrium and mezzanine bridge
8. Material detail: Inspect timber, stone, metal joints, fabric and glass close-up
9. The staircase: Ascend actual staircase with strong foreground framing
10. The workspace: Traverse furnished studio with full-height windows
11. The courtyard: Exit to planted courtyard for quiet open-air pause
12. The sky level: Travel upper corridor to rooftop panoramic terrace
13. Architectural exploded view: Pull out; floors separate vertically and facades move outward; annotations return
14. Deconstruction: Original architectural components progressively align into letter strokes
15. MAJENTA: Original components form uppercase MAJENTA, hold sculptural composition then minimal footer
